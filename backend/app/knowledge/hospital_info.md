# Aranya Multispecialty Hospital — Approved Patient Information

> DEMO DATA. Aranya Multispecialty Hospital is a fictional hospital. Every name,
> number, timing and policy below is invented for this prototype and must be
> replaced and approved by the real hospital before any use.

## Contact and location
- Address: 14 Lakeview Road, Hebbal, Bengaluru 560024.
- Landmark: opposite Hebbal Lake main gate. Nearest metro: Hebbal (about 10 minutes by auto).
- Reception phone: 080-4000-1000.
- Emergency department (casualty) phone: 080-4000-1999. Open 24 hours, all days.
- Ambulance in India: dial 108. National emergency number: 112.
- Parking: basement parking, free for the first 2 hours.

## Reception hours
- Monday to Saturday: 8:00 am to 8:00 pm.
- Sunday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm.
- Outside these hours, appointment requests and callback requests are queued and handled when reception opens.

## Outpatient (OPD) timings
- Morning session: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm.
- Evening session: 4:00 pm to 7:00 pm.
- Saturday: morning session only.
- Sunday: no OPD. Emergency only.
- Patients should arrive 20 minutes early for registration.

## OPD departments and days
- General Medicine: Monday to Saturday.
- Cardiology (heart): Monday to Saturday.
- Orthopaedics (bones and joints): Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday.
- Paediatrics (children): Monday to Saturday.
- Obstetrics and Gynaecology: Monday to Saturday.
- ENT (ear, nose, throat): Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday.
- Diabetes and Endocrinology: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Ophthalmology (eyes): Monday to Friday.
- The hospital does NOT have Dermatology, Psychiatry, Oncology or Neurosurgery OPDs.
- Specific doctor allocation is done by reception when they confirm the appointment.

## Appointments
- Appointment requests can be made through this assistant, by phone, or at reception.
- A request is not a confirmed appointment. Reception calls back to confirm the exact time and doctor.
- To request an appointment we need: department, preferred date, morning or evening, patient's name, and a contact phone number.
- First consultation fee: Rs 600 (General Medicine), Rs 800 (all specialist departments). Follow-up within 7 days is free.
- Payment: cash, UPI, and debit/credit cards at the billing counter.

## What to bring
- Any government photo ID of the patient.
- Previous prescriptions, discharge summaries and test reports.
- List of current medicines.
- Insurance card or TPA details, if using insurance.

## Test preparation (general instructions)
- Fasting blood sugar and lipid profile: no food for 10 to 12 hours before the test. Plain water is allowed.
- Ultrasound abdomen: no food for 6 hours before the scan.
- Ultrasound pelvis: drink about 1 litre of water one hour before and do not pass urine until the scan.
- Patients on regular medicines (including blood thinners or diabetes medicines) must ask their doctor before skipping or changing any dose for a test. Reception cannot advise on this.

## Lab and reports
- Laboratory sample collection: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 am to 7:00 pm. Sunday 7:00 am to 11:00 am.
- Most routine blood reports are ready the same evening. Reports can be collected at the lab counter with the bill receipt.
- Reports cannot be shared through this assistant.

## Visiting hours (inpatients)
- 11:00 am to 12:00 noon, and 5:00 pm to 7:00 pm.
- Two visitors per patient at a time. Children under 12 are not allowed in wards.
- ICU visiting: 5:00 pm to 5:30 pm, one visitor only.

## Insurance
- Cashless treatment is available with major insurers and TPAs. The insurance desk is next to the main reception, open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.
- This assistant cannot check whether a specific policy is covered. The insurance desk can.
